Privacy Policy for Valikati

Effective Date: March 20, 2026

Welcome to Valikati. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Valikati mobile application.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Selected Rasi (Zodiac Sign)
  • Date of Birth (if provided)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token
  • Device Information (device model, operating system, app version)
  • AI Chat Messages (used to provide AI Jothidar responses)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Personalize astrology predictions
  • Display Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly Rasi Palan
  • Provide Panchangam, Nalla Neram, and Mugurtham information
  • Send push notifications
  • Improve AI Jothidar responses
  • Improve the performance and security of the application

Push Notifications

Valikati uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send:

  • Daily Horoscope Updates
  • Festival Notifications
  • Important Announcements
  • Personalized notifications based on your selected Rasi

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

AI Services

The AI Jothidar feature uses AI technology to generate astrology guidance based on the information you provide.

AI-generated responses are intended for informational and entertainment purposes only and should not be considered professional financial, legal, or medical advice.

Data Storage

Your information is securely stored using:

  • MongoDB
  • Prisma ORM
  • Secure Express.js Backend
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging

We use reasonable security measures to protect your personal information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your information may only be shared with trusted third-party services necessary to operate the app, including:

  • Firebase (Push Notifications)
  • AI Service Provider
  • Cloud Hosting Provider

Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations.

Your Rights

You may request to:

  • View your personal information
  • Update your information
  • Delete your account
  • Delete your stored data

To make such a request, please contact us using the email below.

Children's Privacy

Valikati is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app or on our website.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: support@valikati.com
